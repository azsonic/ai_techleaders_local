## Decision summary

The Product owner chose real paid 25/50-minute text sessions, booked at least two hours ahead, reachable through mentor links and a searchable directory. Mentors may stay link-only (D06); the directory lists the soonest available slot first (Q12). Two hours is a floor with no promised start time; the booking-to-start time is recorded (D20). Hours are 10:00–22:00 Europe/Warsaw on all days and stay a hard limit for the pilot (D07, D17, D24), and a charge happens only for a confirmed booking (R06). Mentors set prices within 90–600 PLN (25 min) and 180–1200 PLN (50 min), with a 20% commission (D08, R05). Bookings are auto-confirmed with the mentee's question; the charge is taken at booking, and a mentor no-show or cancellation is refunded the same day (D09, R04). Sessions are in-product text with GitHub sign-in (D10). Paid builds use real Stripe, with mocks only in tests (D13, R07).

The interviews support specific cases of costly delays obtaining expertise. They do not establish demand for this exact offer: two developers wanted help within 20–60 minutes, the freelancer wanted a conversation for estimates, and a platform developer at a large company would pay only for a once-only, day-long task that the company expenses. No mentor coverage is committed: all 50 prospects are recorded as not contacted.

The pilot's success, pause and hard kill rules are chosen targets, not baselines (D11, D15). A supply gate comes first: a personal heads-up goes to 20 mentors this week, the in-product invitations follow when the join page exists, and if fewer than 5 mentors publish availability within two weeks of accepting, further invitations stop and paid booking does not open (D16, R06). Published means both prices, at least one stack, and a bookable slot at least two hours ahead (D24). The invitation text and first-profile check do not exist yet (Q10). Because the clock runs from acceptance, paid booking cannot open on 31 Oct; whether that ship is closed or slips is open (Q15). Two hours is a floor, not a promise: a mentee may book any slot starting at least two hours out, and DevMentor reports the booking-to-start time (D20).

This brief records chosen scope, not implementation readiness. Now is the full marketplace and the 25-hour budget covers a first cut only (D14); the Tech Lead's estimate is due this week (Q08). Payments run in test mode until a legal, tax and invoicing owner is named (D18, Q11). The hard kill rule is written (D15); what counts as a restart is not (Q14).
