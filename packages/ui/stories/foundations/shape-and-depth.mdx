# Shape and depth

The references decide how surfaces separate, not how round they are.

## Decided

| Rule                                                                                                                                  | Source                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Adjacent rows are separated by a thin line, 1px (`--border-width-divider`) in `--border`.                                             | R01.3                        |
| Surfaces are opaque. A panel is distinguished by its border, not by blur or translucency.                                             | A01.2                        |
| A shadow is never the only way to separate content.                                                                                   | A01 (generic AI look), A01.2 |
| Controls and boundaries use full-opacity `--border`, because the fill difference between surfaces is too small to identify a control. | WCAG 1.4.11                  |

## Radii

The radius scale is the shadcn default, derived from one `--radius` (0.625rem) and not yet reviewed against the references. They are shown here as they exist.

## Approved proposals

No reference decides these. The Product owner approved them on 2026-10-25.

- `--radius` stays at 0.625rem. (Component-level use is confirmed in stage 2.)
- Nothing in the system uses a shadow. Popovers and dialogs separate by border and an opaque surface.
