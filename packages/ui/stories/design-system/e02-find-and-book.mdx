# E02 Find and book

A mentee finds a mentor by link or from the directory, sees both prices and the nearest slot, picks a length and a slot, signs in, asks a question and books. Both people are notified, and each sees their bookings and can cancel. Source: `.ai/specs/2026-10-24-find-and-book.md`, decisions D05, D06, D08, D09, D10, D17, D20, D21, D22, D28, D31, D33, D34, rules R04, R05, R08, R09, R10, R12, R15, R17.

## Flow

1. The mentee opens the directory or a mentor's link (**Directory**, **Mentor page**). While paid booking is closed, prices stay visible and a fixed sentence replaces the slot picker.
2. The mentee sees both prices, chooses a length and a slot (**Mentor page**).
3. The mentee signs in if needed and returns to the same selection, then writes a question and books (**Booking page**).
4. Both sides get a notice in the product and by e-mail (**Notifications**).
5. The mentee sees and cancels bookings (**My bookings**); the mentor sees booked sessions and cancels with a reason (**Mentor bookings**).

## Product work that remains

- The pages are not built in the application; these stories are their compositions.
- **Not designed yet, and recorded for "What is missing":** a booking's own page `/bookings/[id]` (session link once E04 lands); the unread count in the header (the application shell is not in the plan); a search field with results; paging for a long directory; the sign-in page.
- The cancellation outcome (`full-grace`, `full`, `none`) is computed by the booking policy in `core`; the dialog only words it. The words say "due when payments process" until E03-S02.
- Notification e-mails use Europe/Warsaw with the zone label; they are not part of this system.
