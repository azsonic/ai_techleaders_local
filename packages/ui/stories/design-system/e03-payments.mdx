# E03 Payments

A mentee pays at booking, a refund or a payout follows the rules, and a mentor sees what they receive. **No E03 specification exists yet.** The components and screens here are proposals drawn from the brief: decisions D08, D09, D13, D18, D22, D32, D33 and rules R04, R05, R07, R09, R15, R16. Each needs approval before it becomes a requirement. Backlog: E03-S01 (pay at booking), E03-S02 (refunds, cancellations and no-shows), E03-S03 (a mentor receives their price minus 20%).

## Flow

1. The mentee picks a slot and writes a question (E02), then reaches the **Payment step**: summary, test-mode notice, Stripe's payment details, "Pay 120 PLN and book". Money is taken only for a confirmed booking (R06).
2. A cancellation or an absence ends in a money outcome worded by **Refund outcome line**: full refund, no refund, or the same-day refund when the mentor cancels or does not attend (R04, R09, R15, R16).
3. The mentor sees each paid session in **Mentor earnings** with the price, the 20% commission and what they receive; a payout is held until the 24-hour report window closes (R05, R16).

## Product work that remains

- **Not designed, recorded for "What is missing":** Stripe's payment form and its field errors (hosted by Stripe); the mentor's Stripe Connect onboarding and payout details (E03-S03-T01 is open); the receipt or invoice; payment history for the mentee; a refund shown on the booking's own page.
- The outcome (`grace`, `full`, `late`, ...) is computed by the booking policy in `core`; the component only words it. The commission is 20% of a whole-zloty price, so it is always a whole number of grosze.
- "Test mode" wording stays until a legal, tax and invoicing owner is named (D18, R07, Q11).
- Status names (Held, Paid, Refunded, In dispute) and the two totals on the earnings page are proposals.
