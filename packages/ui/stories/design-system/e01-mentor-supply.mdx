# E01 Mentor supply

An invited mentor signs in, sets up a profile, chooses to be listed or link-only, and publishes availability. The operator creates the invitations. Source: [E01 spec](https://github.com/azsonic/ai_techleaders_local/blob/main/.ai/specs/2026-10-24-mentor-supply.md) (`.ai/specs/2026-10-24-mentor-supply.md`), decisions D05, D06, D07, D08, D23, D24, rules R05, R08, R11.

## Flow

1. The operator creates an invitation and copies the link, shown once (**Admin invitations**).
2. The mentor opens the link (**Join**), signs in if needed and accepts.
3. The mentor fills the profile and saves it (**Profile**). Listing stays off until both prices and a stack are saved.
4. The mentor adds availability windows and sees what mentees could book (**Availability**).
5. The mentor copies the share link (**Profile**, share link). Mentees reach the page in E02.

## Product work that remains

- The pages themselves (`/join/[token]`, `/mentor/profile`, `/mentor/availability`, `/admin/invitations`, `/mentors/[slug]`) are not built in the application; these stories are their compositions.
- **Not designed yet, and recorded for "What is missing":** the mentor's public work links (up to five, https only); the sign-in page; the revoke action with a required reason (operator action pattern, batch D, R13); where focus goes after a window is removed.
- The price bounds and stacks live in `components/mentors/pricing.ts` until `@devmentor/core` has its mentor schemas.
- Messages for the server's field errors are written by the server; this system only shows them.
